# ChoreQuest portfolio images

The three screenshots are captured from the native SwiftUI app on an iPhone 17 Pro simulator. They use an isolated screenshot build with fictional data, without reading or changing a live family account.

- Family: The Adventure Crew
- Heroes: Maya and Noah
- Quests: Make your bed, Water the plants, Set the dinner table, Pack your school bag, Tidy up your toys
- Rewards: Choose movie night, Ice cream outing, A trip to the park
- Shared goal: Family adventure day

`parent.webp`, `hero.webp`, and `rewards.webp` are optimized captures. `app-icon.png` comes from the app's release icon asset. The portfolio labels the app as coming soon; add the verified App Store link when it is released.

## Presentation frame

The website wraps each original capture in a reusable CSS iPhone 18 Pro Max mockup with a Dynamic Island, front camera, rounded display, home indicator, side controls, and metallic enclosure. Device proportions follow Apple's published 78.0 × 163.4 mm body and 1320 × 2868 display. This presentation does not change the simulator provenance of the underlying captures.

Reference: https://www.apple.com/iphone-18-pro/specs/

## Supplied avatars

The screenshots use the supplied Lorelei SVG for the parent, clay SVG for Maya, and voxel-art SVG for Noah. Copies of the original SVGs are retained in `avatars/`. These were installed in the isolated native demo build before recapturing all three screens.
